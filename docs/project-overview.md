# BRS Mobile Application

## Project Overview

BRS is a construction and project accounting platform designed to manage companies, projects, parties, ledgers, vouchers, and financial reports.

The mobile application is a client application for the BRS FastAPI backend and must remain fully aligned with backend business rules and accounting workflows.

The backend is the single source of truth for:

- Authentication
- Company management
- Fiscal year management
- Party management
- Project management
- Ledger management
- Voucher processing
- Accounting validation
- Financial reporting

The frontend is responsible for:

- User experience
- Data entry
- Validation feedback
- Workflow guidance
- Reporting visualization

---

# Business Domain

BRS is intended for:

- Construction Companies
- Contractors
- Project-Based Businesses
- Engineering Firms
- Infrastructure Projects

The system combines:

- Project Management
- Financial Accounting
- Cost Tracking
- Party Management
- Reporting

into a single platform.

# Accounting Principles

BRS follows double-entry accounting.

Fundamental rule:

```txt
Total Debit = Total Credit
```

Voucher validation occurs on backend.

Frontend may display totals but must not become the source of truth.

---

# Voucher Numbering

Voucher numbers are generated exclusively by backend.

Frontend must never:

- Generate voucher numbers
- Generate sequence numbers
- Generate fiscal year numbering

Frontend only displays values returned by APIs.

---

# Ledger Rules

Ledger balances are maintained by backend.

Frontend must never:

- Calculate running balances
- Store accounting balances
- Simulate posting logic

Always retrieve accounting information from backend APIs.

---

# Reporting Rules

Reports are generated by backend.

Frontend responsibilities:

- Filters
- Date selection
- Presentation
- Export actions (future)

Frontend must not reproduce accounting calculations locally.
